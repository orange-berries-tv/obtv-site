Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 • Brand & Digital Entity: Orange Berries TV

1. Introduction

Welcome to Orange Berries TV ("we", "our", or "us"). We create soothing lullabies, gentle nursery piano melodies, and bedtime visual content designed for young children, infants, and their families.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information across our official website, digital channels, and our official integrations with third-party platforms such as Pinterest, YouTube, Instagram, and Facebook. Your privacy and the safety of your family are of utmost importance to us.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to deliver and operate our content and services:

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

When you contact us via email, submit feedback, or interact with our social media pages, we may receive your name, email address, and any message content you choose to provide.

B. Automatically Collected Technical Information

When you browse our website, standard web server logs may automatically record non-personally identifiable technical data, including your browser type, device information, operating system, and page visit timestamps.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect strictly for legitimate, transparent purposes:

  • To maintain, operate, and enhance our website performance and user experience.
  • To respond to your inquiries, customer support requests, and community feedback.
  • To analyze aggregated, non-identifying traffic patterns to understand which lullabies and characters families enjoy most.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information under any circumstances.

We may only share information in the following limited and necessary situations:

  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party hosting, infrastructure, and delivery services (such as cloud infrastructure) that assist in serving our website.
  • Official Platform Integrations: Official API services (including Pinterest and YouTube) strictly in accordance with their respective developer terms and user-granted permissions.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, subpoena, or valid legal process to protect our rights, safety, and compliance.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website does not use invasive advertising cookies or cross-site tracking pixels. Third-party platforms linked from our site (such as YouTube or Pinterest) may use their own cookies when you visit their external websites or interact with embedded media players, governed by their respective privacy policies.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Protecting children's online safety is our highest priority.

While Orange Berries TV produces family-friendly bedtime content enjoyed by babies and toddlers under parental supervision, our website is intended for parents, guardians, and educators. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal information from children under the age of 13, in compliance with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy standards.

If you believe that a child has inadvertently provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly delete such data from our records.

7. Data Security & Retention

We implement rigorous technical, organizational, and administrative safeguards to protect any data we process against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. All API authentication tokens are stored using industry-standard local encryption. We retain information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or to comply with statutory obligations.

8. Your Rights and Data Control

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, update, or request the deletion of your personal data.

If you have authorized our publishing integration on Pinterest or YouTube, you may revoke access at any time directly through your platform account settings:

  • Pinterest Apps Management: pinterest.com/settings/apps
  • Google Security Permissions: myaccount.google.com/permissions

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect improvements in our practices or changes in legal requirements. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised "Effective Date" at the top. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
